Crooked Dice Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Crooked Dice ("the game") is made by Jacob Drake ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (such as runs played, unlocks and settings) is stored only on your device, and deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software may collect information from your device. This can include the advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), your device's IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and interactions with ads.

On iOS, the game asks for your permission before any tracking, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In regions where consent is required (such as the EU, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your choices before showing ads. You can change them later with the "Ad privacy choices" button in the pause menu (☰).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
